# Embryo Chip Viewer

Embryo Chip Viewer is Embryo-Zero's fork of [KLayout](https://github.com/KLayout/klayout) (v0.30.13, commit 06b8ff4). It views chip layouts and runs rule checks (DRC) and wiring checks (LVS).
It is one of the Embryo chip tools, with Embryo Router (our Freerouting fork) and Embryo PCB (our KiCad fork).
